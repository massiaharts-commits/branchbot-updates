FAQ

Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know.

Does Branch Bot work offline? ▼
Yes! Branch Bot runs 100% locally on your computer. You can use all features without an internet connection. AI features require an API key and internet, but everything else works offline.
Can I export my data? ▼
Absolutely. You can export your entire graph as JSON, export individual nodes, or create interactive HTML presentations. Your data is stored in standard SQLite databases that you can access anytime.
What's BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)? ▼
BYOK plans let you use your own Claude API key from Anthropic. This saves you $3-4/month on subscription cost, but you pay Anthropic directly for API usage. Great for power users who want more control.
Is my data secure? ▼
Yes. Branch Bot runs entirely on your local machine — your data never touches our servers. No cloud storage, no syncing to external services. You have complete control and privacy.
What if I hit my node limit? ▼
You'll see a friendly notification when approaching your limit. You can upgrade your plan anytime, or delete unused nodes to make room. The free tier gives you 200 nodes to start!
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? ▼
Yes, you can change plans anytime through your Gumroad account. Changes take effect immediately.
